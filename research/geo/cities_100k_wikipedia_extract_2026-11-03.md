This table lists the 348 [incorporated places](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipal_corporation "Municipal corporation") in the United States, excluding the [U.S. territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territories_of_the_United_States "Territories of the United States"), with a population of at least 100,000 as of July 1, 2025, as estimated by the [U.S. Census Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census_Bureau "United States Census Bureau"). Five states have no cities with populations exceeding 100,000: [Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delaware "Delaware"), [Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine "Maine"), [Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont "Vermont"), [West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia "West Virginia"), and [Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyoming "Wyoming").
